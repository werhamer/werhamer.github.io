🌱 Ritenza — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026 · Version 2.0.0

Your personal habit data never leaves your device. Ritenza's core tracking features work entirely offline and use no account, ads, analytics, or developer server. If you choose to buy Plus, payment is processed only by Google Play or Apple under their own terms.

What is stored?

Everything you create while using the app is stored only in your phone's own storage:

  • Your habits and completion records
  • Your daily notes and mood entries
  • Your profile (name, avatar, goal) — only what you type in
  • App settings (theme, language, reminders)

This data lives in the device's standard app storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

What is not collected?

Data type Status
Personal identifiers (name, email, phone) Not collected
Location Not accessed
Contacts, photos, camera, microphone Not accessed
Device or advertising identifiers Not read
Usage analytics, crash reporting None
Advertising SDKs None

Permissions and why they are requested

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Only to show the reminders you set yourself
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM So reminders arrive at the time you chose
VIBRATE Touch feedback (can be turned off in settings)
com.android.vending.BILLING To buy or restore Plus and verify entitlement with Google Play
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED / WAKE_LOCK To keep local reminders working after the phone restarts
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS To play optional in-app completion sounds through the correct audio output

If you decline the notification permission, the rest of the app keeps working fully — you simply won't receive reminders.

Data sharing

Ritenza does not share your habits, journals, moods, or private notes with any third party. When you initiate a backup export or a badge/stats share, the content goes through your phone's share sheet to the app you select. From that point the receiving app's privacy policy applies. Ritenza never starts this on its own.

When you purchase Plus, Google Play or Apple processes the payment and payment method directly; Ritenza never sees your card or bank details. The app stores only the store product identifier and whether your Plus entitlement is valid in secure storage on your device. This information is not sent to a developer server.

Android's automatic app backup is disabled: app data is never copied to Google Drive or any other cloud. A backup exists only when you export one, and you decide where it goes.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Reset All Data — permanently deletes everything, including your profile
  • By uninstalling: all data is removed from the device

Because we hold none of your data, there is no deletion request to send.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above is revised. For a material change — such as beginning to collect data — you will be informed inside the app.
