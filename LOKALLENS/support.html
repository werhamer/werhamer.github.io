LokalLens Support

Offline photo and document analysis for iPhone.

Quick help

  • Use Choose photo for an item from the system photo picker.
  • Use Scan document for a multi-page camera scan.
  • Use Import PDF to analyze a PDF stored in Files.
  • Saved results remain in the local archive until you delete them.
