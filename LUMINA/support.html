Lumina — Support

Light Circuits — an offline puzzle game.

How to play

Rotate tiles to carry light from the source to every required endpoint. A level is complete when every path is connected. Long-press a tile to rotate it in the opposite direction.

Progress and hints

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it. Lumina offers three free hints each day; unused hints do not carry over.

Need help?
