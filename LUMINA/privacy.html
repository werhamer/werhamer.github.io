Lumina — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Lumina collects no personal data. It has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, analytics, or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, unlocked themes, and daily-puzzle state stay only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not sent to a server and are removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

Internet and third parties

Lumina is fully playable offline. It makes no network requests and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, billing, or social-media services.
