Puzzle Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Puzzle Master currently does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game works offline and does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, best scores, theme and accessibility preferences are stored locally on your device. Removing the app removes this local data, subject to your operating system backup settings.

Permissions

The optional vibration setting uses your device’s haptic capability only to provide touch feedback. The game does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Advertising and analytics

Version 1.0 includes no advertising SDK, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated server. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Because the current version collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
