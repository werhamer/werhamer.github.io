✨ Lumina — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Lumina does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. The puzzle game works offline with no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or developer server.

What is stored on your device?

Lumina keeps only the game information needed to continue playing, in your device's local app storage:

  • Level progress, stars, and best results
  • Your selected language and basic game settings

This information stays on your device. Removing the app removes its local app container. Your operating system may include app data in a device backup according to your own Apple or Google backup settings.

What is not collected?

Data typeStatus
Name, email, phone, or account detailsNot collected
Location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphoneNot accessed
Advertising or device identifiersNot read
Usage analytics or crash reportingNone
Advertising SDKs or third-party trackersNone

Data sharing

Lumina does not share data with third parties because it does not collect or transmit data to the developer.

Children's privacy

Lumina collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its update date will be revised before the change takes effect.
